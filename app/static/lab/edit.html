How sizes work

Every size is a formula of the piece's overall size, in millimetres: L = left to right, W = front to back, H = height. For example a leg's length can be H - 30, a rail (L - 80) / 2. You can use + - * /, brackets and numbers. Each row is one placed piece: for four legs add four rows (use Copy). Axis says which way the piece's length runs. The position is the piece's lowest corner (x left, y up, z front).

Hardware

Screws, nails, dowels, bolts, brackets, glue and clamps. The quantity is a formula like the sizes (for example L / 150) and is rounded up to a whole number. Clamps are tools, not stock: the Lab lists them under the build step they hold. Step is optional.

Mark a carved area on a photo

Pick a photo of the piece and mark one carved area: click its four corners in order (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left), or type them below. Then, if the shape is not a rectangle (a crown, say), click points around it to cut it out. The workshop PC straightens the area, reads its depth, and it appears under Designs from scans when ready. Mark the border, the lattice and the medallion one at a time. A photo taken straight on is best; the closer the area fills the photo, the better.

Designs from scans

A finished flat-carving scan of this piece has a depth picture. Press Make a design to file it under CNC designs as a furniture design (its picture, and the G-code if the scan made it), ready to carve below and to cut later. It only happens when you press the button. Pressing it again, or for a scan whose picture is already a design, gives that design back instead of a copy. Owner only.

Carvings

Put a design on a face of a part. The design and its picture are stored once under CNC designs; use the same design on as many parts as you like (Copy, then change the part, and tick Mirror for the other side). Make a new design, with its picture, in CNC designs first. Placing a design here files it under furniture there. The picture is centred on the face, scaled to fit inside the margin. Save the parts first: you can only pick parts that are saved.
